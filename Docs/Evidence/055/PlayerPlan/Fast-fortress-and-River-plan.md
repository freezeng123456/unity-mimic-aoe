## Coast B10 continuation

Scope: Coast, normal opening, Easy, age cap III, legal Economy 25. Coast starts with 300F300W100C in its account, so opening crates bring the total to 600F600W100C. A 135W Manor grants one worker on completion. Coast's relevant deck cards include New Settlers (two workers), Coin Convoy 2 (600C), and age III Hussar Reserves (four hussars). See [Rules.json:2723](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L2723), [Economy.cs:193](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/Economy.cs#L193), [Rules.json:2219](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L2219), [Rules.json:2558](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L2558).

From the operator-reported 104-second checkpoint:

1. Pay 800F for age II with the economic governor. Use eight workers on two deer, four per deer, and four on the nearby mine. Hold further worker, Manor, and technology spending. With no queue delay, II completes at approximately 194 seconds. Before travel or interruption losses, the 90-second interval yields about 605F and 216C; after collecting the 200F reward, the account would be approximately 824F543W316C.
2. Send **Coin Convoy 2, 600C**, as soon as II and a ticket are available. Take one food worker to construct the Barracks, then pay for **one musketeer**. This costs 200W75F25C and supplies a separately paid infantry unit without committing to an unaffordable army.
3. Collect the coin crate promptly: four workers need 25 seconds of actual gathering; eight need 12.5 seconds. Mine until the age III payment is covered, retaining a small coin buffer if possible. Return surplus miners to deer until the account has 1200F1000C after paying for the musketeer. The combined food target before both payments is 1275F, and combined coin target is 1025C.
4. Queue III with the economic governor. During its 110 seconds, put four workers back on the mine: uninterrupted work produces 264C, enough for the paid cannon. Allow for walking or use a coin buffer; do not permanently stop mining after funding the age.
5. At III, collect the 200W reward and use four workers to build the Foundry. Queue one paid cannon when 100W250C and four population are available. The wood budget is **543 - 200 + 200 - 300 - 100 = 143W remaining**. This route does not require additional chopping unless defense or replacements consume that margin.
6. When the next ticket is actually earned, consider four Hussar Reserves as the cannon escort. Their eight population plus 12 workers, one musketeer, and one cannon total 25 population, within one Manor plus TC capacity of 30. Do not assume a third military shipment before its ticket exists.

A cannon birth around 470–530 seconds is a planning estimate with allowance for manual execution, not an observed event or guaranteed deadline. The economy has room to pursue building damage before 800 seconds, but attack timing, losses, and collection delays determine whether it works.

Use TC shelter only while it is needed, then release workers and restore their gather orders. A lone musketeer and TC are a minimum budget, not proof that every wave is survivable. If a visible attack demands more defense, preserve workers and revise the timetable.

For the attack, plain Move orders suppress automatic fighting while they are active. Give an explicit attack on a visible enemy building after the cannon reaches position. The cannon has range 20, minimum range 4, vision 18, and a two-second deployment delay; use an explorer or escort to maintain vision and protect it from close units. The public far-west route `(8,28) -> (8,110) -> (97,114)` is only an untested flank proposal. See [MovementCombat.cs:34](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/MovementCombat.cs#L34), [MovementCombat.cs:54](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/MovementCombat.cs#L54), and [World.cs:53](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/World.cs#L53).

## River sequence toward age V

This is a separate proposed game with cap V. It is not a continuation of Coast's economy or a report of any River milestones.

River starts with 100F300W400C in its account; opening crates bring the total to **400F600W400C**. Workers cost 100C each and take 20 seconds. Houses cost 100W and grant no worker. The default bank limit is four; each completed bank costs 350F350W, takes 70 seconds with one builder or 28 seconds with four, and produces 2.5C per second. Bank income grants no gathering XP. See [Rules.json:2738](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L2738), [Progression.cs:7](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/Progression.cs#L7), [Rules.json:841](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L841), and [Economy.cs:182](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/Economy.cs#L182).

River Economy 25 has no Coast New Settlers or age II Musketeer Company. Its only age I card is River Coin (250C); its age II military card is Mounted Guard (two hussars). At III it includes five musketeers, six pikemen, six carabineers, four hussars, and one cannon among its available military shipments. Tickets still have to be earned; bank income cannot finance them through XP.

1. **Opening and II:** buy four workers to reach ten using the opening 400C. Attempt the same public treasures. Send River Coin first. Keep the TC queue clear when ready to advance; select the II economic reward and collect its 200F.
2. **Stabilize:** build bank 1, a Barracks, and one house. Including the 75W treasure, their wood budget is **675 - 350 - 200 - 100 = 25W**. Send Coin Convoy 2 as the second economic shipment when available. Pay for one batch of five musketeers, 375F125C, with short TC garrisons as needed. These are purchases in addition to the age III target, not part of it.
3. **Reach III:** save 1200F1000C and choose the 200W economic reward. Use some mining while bank 1 accumulates coin; do not assume that a newly completed single bank funds the age immediately. Stop adding workers if doing so consumes the next age or immediate defense budget.
4. **Create pressure:** prefer Wood Convoy 3 (1000W) for the next available ticket. Foundry 300W, Stable 200W, and two paid cannons 200W500C leave **25 + 200 + 1000 - 300 - 200 - 200 = 525W** before other spending. The Stable purchase does not include cavalry: buy escorts only with an additional explicit resource budget. Keep the five musketeers screening the cannons; use earned military shipments when they fit the actual ticket schedule.
5. **Contain before expanding:** two unupgraded cannons deal 260 building damage per five-second volley. A full 3500HP TC takes fourteen volleys, approximately 65–70 seconds of uninterrupted cannon fire. Hold infantry outside unnecessary TC fire and address visible cavalry, artillery, and production buildings. Travel, target loss, deployment, enemy repairs, and fighting extend this time.
6. **Add bank 2 and workers:** the 525W margin can fund bank 2's 350W, but its 350F is an additional expense. Once control permits, grow to 18–20 workers and add housing before population blocks production. Preserve **2000F1200C** for IV; it takes 130 seconds. Food Convoy 3 can help if a ticket is available.
7. **IV to V:** choose IV's 600W400C reward. If the earlier 175W margin remains, it plus 600W covers banks 3 and 4, which cost another **700F700W**. Four banks produce **10C per second** even while workers shelter. Save **4000F4000C** for V, which takes 160 seconds. Use unspent 1000F/1400F/1400C shipments according to the actual bottleneck and earned tickets. Do not spend the V reward before V has completed. See [Rules.json:2689](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L2689), [Rules.json:2415](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L2415), and [Rules.json:2514](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L2514).

Bank expansion, houses, escort units, replacement troops, and technology all require their own budgets. Defer bank-limit upgrades, bank-income technology, and farms until they solve an observed constraint; the bank route is not an unlimited resource or shipment source.

## Completion and survival risks

- Killing the enemy TC does not permanently disable it. The AI tries to rebuild when it has workers and can fund a TC; a TC costs 500W. Continue observing the visible production area. See [SkirmishAI.cs:41](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/SkirmishAI.cs#L41) and [Rules.json:479](../../../../Assets/Resources/Rules.json#L479).
- Easy changes reaction cadence, not these resource or combat budgets. AI expeditions can form from six soldiers in ages I–II and ten in III; these are policy thresholds, not evidence of the current enemy army. See [SkirmishAI.cs:146](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/SkirmishAI.cs#L146).
- If time is needed to reach V after gaining control, keep an already observed surviving enemy building outside army firing range and avoid automatic cleanup orders. This does not guarantee safety or prevent rebuilding. Do not rely on an unseen survivor.
- Natural victory requires eliminating every opposing unit and building; resource crates do not keep a player alive. Only the actual native result can establish natural completion or any required paid-unit and damage milestone. See [World.cs:176](../../../../Assets/Frontier/Simulation/World.cs#L176).
