Two separable causes remain: **A)** the exact query is below water at its strip boundary but the 1m triangle interpolant rises above it; **B)** the strip boundary itself is dry/high under the signed-distance authority. Scenery shadows are an additional visual confound, controlled by keeping the same scene or a diagnostic shadow-off pair, not by changing gameplay.

## Minimal next controlled probe / candidate fix

First record A/B measurements below on the actual generated meshes. If A dominates, the smallest reviewable production candidate is **Winding-only ground sampling at .5m (257²)** with all BankHeight/BankLayers calls, UV scale, material, channel/water vertices, bridge transforms and authoritative navigation unchanged. No new collider, channel widening, depth bias, or signed-distance change. Regular refinement has no adaptive T-junction stitching risk. Its aligned original 1m vertices remain an exact subset of the new mesh.

Cost is explicit: 16,641→66,049 ground vertices, 32,768→131,072 triangles for Winding; existing UInt32 indexing handles >65,535 vertices. Do not claim performance neutrality or solve it by silently adding the same cost to Tropical/Tributaries/Estuary/generated/scenario maps. Dense .25m full-map geometry is not the first proposal. Start with one exact .5m controlled comparison; **if remaining clearance violations/notches persist, do not call it fixed** or keep turning up density without evidence.

If B dominates or .5m fails the same test, investigate a next separate **contour-conforming shore strip**: derive both visible boundary and ground seam from roots of the existing SignedBankDistance, leaving its water/land classification untouched. This is a larger topology/welding change and not bundled into the first density trial. Expanding water width to hide gaps would put visible water onto authoritative dry land; lowering BankHeight would change map authority/protection contracts. Neither is an acceptable disguised presentation-only fix.
