# Normal player evidence — 0.2.9

This document is a post-packaging evidence supplement for the 0.2.9 native client. It was collected after the sealed 0.2.9 package and is not included in that sealed package. It will be delivered with the next source version. The authoritative raw files are `native-player-match_finished.json`, `native-player-match_finished-events.jsonl`, `NativePlayer.log`, and `native-player-quit.json` in this verification directory.

The match used the normal local-player path with real mouse and keyboard input, Coast against the mirrored Coast AI, seed `314159`, Age III cap, simulation speed 1, and no autoplay. It began with the configured `300 food / 300 wood / 100 coin`, six settlers, and one Pathfinder. The client was the 0.2.9 build with GUID `4e851ef57c2f460885559d364e1edf6e`, Unity `6000.6.0f1`, running on the Apple M4 Pro at 2560×1440.

The player reached Age II at `256.8s` and Age III at `591.5s`. Four actual shipments were sent and arrived: New Settlers (`125.399 → 160.401s`), Coin Convoy 2 (`287.908 → 322.910s`), Cannon Support (`620.596 → 655.587s`), and Coin Convoy 3 (`758.962 → 793.953s`). The event ledger records `4596.499` food, `1994.482` wood, and `2694.212` coin gathered; spending was `4325/1470/1625` for food/wood/coin. These values come from the simulation report and do not by themselves establish optimal economic play.

The player did one tactical pause at approximately `891s`; the native operation also contains tool-operated waits and input intervals. This is therefore evidence of a completed normal-input attempt with real keyboard and mouse interaction, not a claim of uninterrupted, unaided, fluent human play.

The free Cannon Support arrived at `655.587s` and spawned Field Cannon entity 214. A paid Field Cannon payment occurred at `725.270s` for `100 wood / 250 coin`, and the unit spawned as entity 237 at `770.359s`. The two player cannons generated 28 Fire, 23 Impact, and 39 Damage events. The per-target counts recorded in the audit are Fire-event counts and sum to 28; Impact and Damage are separate event classes and are not being treated as equal counts. All cannon target classes were enemy military units or an enemy Field Cannon. There was **no player Damage event against an enemy building**. The two cannons therefore participated in army combat, but this match did not demonstrate artillery damage to a building.

The player Town Center died at `977.508362s`. Play continued for `32.991943s` until the final player entity, Foundry 216, died at `1010.500305s`. The result was a natural `MatchEnded` with player 1 winning; there was no `Resign` event. The player report ended with population 0. The exact last-entity interval is evidence that the game did not treat the first Town Center death as immediate victory.

After the result, the `PLAY AGAIN` path was visually exercised (`player-result-play-again.jpeg`). The fresh-match report then showed Age I, six population, zero shipments, winner `-1`, time `1.100000s`, and player resources reset to `300/300/100`; the match was subsequently quit. This verifies lifecycle reset state, while the screenshot supplies the UI-path evidence.

This run opens the manual core-loop gate further: the player performed scouting, economy, age advancement, shipments, military production, cannon arrival, and combat in one natural match. It does not close the gate. The player caused no recorded damage to an enemy building, and the run is not evidence that the player completed an artillery siege or won. The broader Stage B manual core-loop requirement remains open; Stage C second-civilization/five-age land content and Stage D final visual, audio, balance, performance, and quality acceptance also remain open.

The next player-combat check should keep a mixed escort alive and deliberately order both the free and paid cannon onto an enemy building, then verify a player-side Damage event whose target entity maps to an enemy building spawn. This is the next concrete missing evidence; no causal explanation for the prior loss is asserted here.
