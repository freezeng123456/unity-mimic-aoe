# Art 087: roads and powder smoke

The caravan road is a continuous mesh with small rounded corners away from water, bridge approaches and barriers. It does not change the simulated caravan route. The road material still has a relatively hard edge and remains an art refinement target.

Ranged fire now has a short, small flash and soft powder smoke, with a larger scale for artillery and vessels. Fog visibility, pooling and the existing effect capacity remain in force. A prior oversized yellow flash was rejected after viewing the real render.

## Evidence and limits

`Docs/Evidence/087/PlayMode.xml` records 16 passing, source-stable PlayMode tests: the four river map render studies, four controlled civilization battle captures, combat and scouting presentation checks, and a controlled live-fire smoke render. `verification.json` binds the run to the source fingerprint. The smoke test generated actual Fire events from accepted attack orders in a controlled spawned-unit scene; it is not a normal paid match, native input or performance test. At 0.1 s after firing, smoke contributed 549 changed pixels for the musket and 1625 for the cannon against a same-frame control with only smoke hidden. Visual review is still required for full battles and naval fire.

The original captured images were placed in the existing private Google Drive folder: [musket](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VOQycuSN8ucb8-4Oacfe470qwpzZLitC/view), [cannon](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1q5FkajpoNgV4Ffo3VaUBMF7ohKVYIFuu/view), and [winding river](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1hPuExxwbbCzs5-BUHwfIYcuBI4k14B5L/view). The images are Editor captures without the HUD, not screenshots of a released native build.

Neutral ally expansions remain an external candidate: their changes to the rules hash affect older saves, scenarios and replays and require a separate compatibility solution and in-engine verification.
